### English

- Overview InflightLiveCard: each account slot bar is divided into N equal cells from that account’s max concurrency (`max_inflight`); occupied cells follow live `in_flight` (wash + elapsed shimmer). One concurrency → one full-width cell. Idle cells stay faint; hairline wash only — keep glow, fail→Failed, ProviderMark, short copy.

### 中文

- Overview「实时进行中」：账号槽进度条按该账号最大并发（`max_inflight`）均分成 N 格；占用格跟 live `in_flight`（洗底 + 耗时流光）。并发为 1 时一整条。空闲格淡色；仅洗底/细描边——保留流光、失败飞 Failed、ProviderMark、精简文案。
