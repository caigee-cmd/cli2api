### English

- The request detail dialog in Logs is regrouped: errors show first as an alert, followed by a summary of status, model, tokens, credits and latency, then call info, attempts and diagnostics. Attempts now show account names and per-attempt latency, and bytes read are formatted.

### 中文

- 日志页的请求详情弹窗重新排版：错误以提示框置顶，接着是状态、模型、Tokens、消耗和延迟的摘要，再分组展示调用信息、尝试记录和诊断。尝试记录改为显示账号名称和每次尝试的耗时，读取字节数也做了格式化。
