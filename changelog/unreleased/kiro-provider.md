### English

- Add an experimental Kiro provider. Import a Builder ID, Identity Center, social, or external sign-in, or paste a ksk_ key. Account credits come from Kiro usage, including an enabled overage allowance, and chat follows the account's CreateResponse or legacy path, including tools, images, and tool_choice. Unpaired tool history is narrated, leaked tool markup is recovered, and web_search is sent as Kiro's webSearch.

### 中文

- 新增实验性的 Kiro 供应商。可导入 Builder ID、Identity Center、社交登录或外部登录凭证，也可以粘贴 ksk_ 密钥。账号额度读取 Kiro 用量，包含已开启的超额额度。聊天按账号走 CreateResponse 或旧接口，并带上工具、图片和 tool_choice。不成对的工具历史会改写成叙述，正文里泄漏的工具标记会还原成调用，web_search 会按 Kiro 的 webSearch 发送。
