### English

- Accounts can be checked in together from one control. Enabled accounts that support check-in are included; accounts already checked in today are skipped, and one failure does not stop the rest. Accounts are checked one at a time with a pause between them, so the pass does not hit every account at once.

### 中文

- 账号页可以一键签到。已启用且支持签到的账号会一起处理；今天已经签到的会跳过，单个失败不会中断其余账号。账号逐个签到，两次之间留有间隔，不会一次把所有账号打满。
