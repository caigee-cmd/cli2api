### English

- Accounts batch import accepts official WorkBuddy client export packs (`exportType: WorkBuddy-accounts`, nested `{account, auth}`), and treats `workbuddy.cn` as the CN region (same as `codebuddy.cn`).

### 中文

- 账号批量导入支持 WorkBuddy 官方客户端导出包（`exportType: WorkBuddy-accounts`、嵌套 `{account, auth}`），并将 `workbuddy.cn` 识别为国内区（与 `codebuddy.cn` 一致）。
