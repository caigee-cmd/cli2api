### English

- Importing a Kiro credential JSON, from the add-account form or the accounts-page batch import, recognizes Builder ID, Identity Center, social, and `ksk_` shapes, including an `accounts` list, and stores each account in the region the file names.

### 中文

- 添加账号里的 JSON 导入和账号页的批量导入都会识别 Kiro 的 Builder ID、Identity Center、社交登录和 `ksk_` 格式，包括 `accounts` 列表，并把每个账号建在文件写明的地区。
